// New add-on

Electrochemical models, one click away

The Electrochemical Models Add-On adds four PyBaMM-based cell engines to Battery Sim Suite. Start with a fast equivalent-circuit model, and step up to physics-based models when you need to see what happens inside the electrodes.

Built for people who work with battery systems

{% include icon.html name="flask" %}

Battery R&D engineers

{% include icon.html name="bolt" %}

EV and storage teams

Compare pack layouts against energy and voltage targets. Run real drive cycles and year-long calendars before committing to a geometry or chemistry.

{% include icon.html name="doc" %}

Researchers and students

Explore SOC trajectories, cell-to-cell spread and model fidelity on your own machine. Every input stays in a local library, so studies are easy to reproduce.

{% include icon.html name="lock" px=28 %}

Local-first, by design

Cells, packs, drive cycles and results are stored on your computer and never uploaded. Simulations run without an internet connection. The only thing the app sends anywhere is licence information, as described in the privacy policy.
