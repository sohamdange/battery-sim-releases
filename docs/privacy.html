Privacy Policy

Last updated 24 August 2026

BatterySimSuite is desktop software for battery pack simulation. This policy explains what information it collects, why, and who else is involved. It is written to be read rather than skimmed, and it describes what the software actually does.

Your simulation work never leaves your computer. Cells, packs, drive cycles, schedules and results are stored in a folder on your own machine (BatterySimSuite in your user directory). They are not uploaded, not backed up to us, and not visible to us. The only thing the software sends anywhere is licence information.

What is collected, and why

InformationWhenWhy
Email address, and optionally your name and organisation When you request a licence key To send you the key, and to identify your licence if you contact support
Licence key Each time the application starts To confirm the licence is valid and has not expired
Hardware identifiers: network adapter address, disk serial number, processor ID, and Windows installation ID Each time the application starts To tie one licence to one computer, so that a key cannot be shared or resold
IP address and the time of each check Each time the application starts To detect misuse, such as a single key being used from many places at once

The hardware identifiers are values your operating system already exposes to installed software. They are stored so that the licence server can tell whether a later request comes from the same machine. They are not used to identify you personally, and they are not combined with anything else.

What is not collected

  • No simulation data, results, cell chemistry, pack designs or drive cycles
  • No file names, folder contents or anything else from your disk
  • No analytics, usage tracking, telemetry or crash reporting
  • No advertising identifiers, and no tracking across other websites or applications

Who else is involved

Three services are used to run the licensing system. None of them receives your simulation work.

  • Railway hosts the licence server and its database, which holds your email address, licence key and the hardware identifiers described above.
  • Google delivers the email containing your licence key, sent from a Gmail account.
  • GitHub hosts the installer downloads and the update files. Downloading an installer or an update reaches GitHub directly.

Your information is not sold, rented, or shared for advertising, and it is not passed to anyone beyond what is described here.

How long it is kept

Licence records are kept for as long as the licence is active, and for a period afterwards so that renewals and support requests can be handled. Check-in records, which show when a licence was verified and from where, are kept only as long as they are useful for detecting misuse.

Your data

You can ask for a copy of the information held about your licence, or for it to be deleted. Deleting it also ends the licence, since the licence is the record. Email soham.perpetual@gmail.com and it will be handled.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the date at the top will change with it. Material changes to what is collected or who it is shared with will be noted on this page rather than made quietly.

Contact

BatterySimSuite is built and maintained by Soham Dange. Questions about this policy, or about what the software does with any particular piece of information, can go to soham.perpetual@gmail.com.
