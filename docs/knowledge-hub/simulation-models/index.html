OCV · R0 · RC pairs · Lookup tables · Inside the cell · SPM · SPMe · DFN

What the video covers

  1. Open-circuit voltage: what the cell reads at rest, set by how full it is.
  2. R0: the instant drop when current flows. At 90 % SOC, 13 mΩ × 5 A = 65 mV straight away.
  3. RC pairs: the slow part. After the current stops the voltage jumps back 89 mV, then creeps up another 26 mV over ten minutes.
  4. Lookup tables: all of it is tabulated by SOC and temperature. R0 falls from 16.5 mΩ at 5 °C to 5.6 mΩ at 55 °C.
  5. Inside the cell: lithium leaves the graphite particles, crosses the electrolyte and enters the cathode.
  6. SPM: one average particle per electrode.
  7. SPMe: adds the salt concentration in the electrolyte.
  8. DFN: particles all through each electrode, coupled to the electrolyte.

Same cell, same cycle: RC took 0.7 s, SPM 6.3 s, SPMe 10.5 s and DFN 22.9 s, and SPMe landed within 8 mV of DFN. The RC model uses the cell’s lab-fitted table and the others the published Chen2020 parameters, so part of RC’s gap is the data. Pick the model that fits the question.
