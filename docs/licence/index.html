// Questions

Licence FAQ

Is it really free?

Yes. Licences are currently issued free of charge, and carry the same terms as any other. Free availability may end at some point, but a licence already issued continues until the date on it. See the terms.

The email has not arrived

Look in your spam or junk folder first. The key comes from {{ site.contact_email }}, an ordinary Gmail account, so filters often catch it. If nothing arrives after a few minutes, submit the form again. It re-sends the same key rather than issuing a new one.

I have a new computer

Email {{ site.contact_email }} from the address the licence was issued to, and it will be moved at no charge.

What happens when it expires?

The app warns you in advance and shows the expiry date in Settings. Ask for an extension any time before or after it ends. Your simulation files stay on your computer either way.

Does the add-on need a separate licence?

No. The Electrochemical Models Add-On is free with any active licence.

Does the app need internet?

The licence is checked when the app starts. A short-lived offline token means brief outages do not interrupt your work, and a licence-server outage is reported as an outage, not as an invalid licence.

What information does licensing use?

Your email (and optionally name and organisation), the key, hardware identifiers that tie the licence to one computer, and the IP address and time of each check. The privacy policy explains each one.
